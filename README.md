# Noghal

Welcome in the world of Noghal! Noghal is a planet rich with fantastic creatures and sizzling magic. The technology, the people use there, is based on magic crystals - the Tar'Nogh stones, which are mined by the dwarves deep in the crystal mountains. The species of the humans, the elves, the dwarves, the lizard-like Krunark and the plant-like Xylanth have come together to a peaceful coexistence.

But dark shadows lurk on the horizon of destiny! Sinister demonic powers are returning to this world! They are allied with some ancient inhabitants of Noghal, who left the planet millennia ago. These inhabitants are technologically far advanced and now return from their exodus to put their feet back onto Noghal again.

## Files

- *Mysterious Discoveries* is a game design document from 2002 in German, which describes a Baldurs Gate like tactical RPG with 4 heroes
- *Forgotten Legends* is a game design document from 2003 in German, which describes a Diablo 2 (but brought to 3rd person) like hack and slay RPG with 1 heroe
- *Dark Elysium* is a game design document from 2018 in English, which is basically the *Forgotten Legends* game design document, but extended a little bit
- *Story of a Universe* is a short story document from 2026 in English, which describes the full Noghal story arc, for the prehistory of Noghal (Age 1-6), Noghal 1 (Age 7, the game design documents), for Noghal 2 (Age 8) and Noghal 3 (Age 9)

## Background

The Noghal universe was mainly developed between 1997 and 2003.
We were a crew of 4 people (+ 2 * 0.25 helpers), who tried to develop a really epic RPG.
At first *Mysterious Discoveries*, then we reworked it to *Forgotten Legends*
But due to financial reasons, the team was abandoned in 2004.
I continued Noghal as a hobby project.
We never made it.
But now, the documents are free for all, so if you want to continue our journey, please do!

BTW: Our team was named *Harbinger Entertainment*. And the word *Harbinger* we got from a dictionary with about 300.000 entries by rolling 26 various dices (from D4 to D20s) for the page and the word.
